Listening screensaver: two styles

Shown only while something is playing, after a few minutes without a key press. Fire OS will not let an app turn its own screensaver off or replace it in system settings, but an app may keep the screen on while it plays; Fahrenheit then shows one of these instead. When nothing plays, the Fire TV screensaver runs as today. The first key press only wakes the screen. Everything moves and is dimmed, so nothing sits still long enough to mark a screen. Both frames below are animated.
