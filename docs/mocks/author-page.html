Author page: the book page's layout, books you can tell apart

Today the author page is its own screen without the side menu: a square photo, the bio as a block of text, and one row of covers with titles only, so two editions of the same book look identical. This version uses the book page's layout (#202/#225): the side menu stays, a round photo and the name on the left, the bio in a box that opens in full on OK, then the books grouped by series in series order, with the rest under "Other books". Each card says what tells it apart: series number, the subtitle when there is one (such as "Unabridged") and the length. It keeps #225's loading frame.
