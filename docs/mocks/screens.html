Fahrenheit TV — mocks for the rest of the screens

All five follow docs/ui-style-guide.md: the box pattern, a 3dp focus border, the 540dp height budget, server strings clipped with an ellipsis. Colours are the app's real Material 3 dark roles, so these look like what the stick renders rather than a palette of mine.

Each frame is a real 1920×1080 screen at half scale. px = dp × 2.

1. The navigation rail #58

The drawer is a phone ModalNavigationDrawer: invisible until opened, and it steals the whole screen when it is. A TV rail is always there — icons at rest, labels when focus enters it — so LEFT from the first column has somewhere to land, which is the half of #53 I could not fix without it. 5. Search #51

Typing on this thing is the worst job on the device — the keyboard covers the middle of the screen and the field gives no sign it has focus. So the field is the arrival focus with a visible caret, results group by kind instead of one undifferentiated list, and each group clips to one row so the whole answer fits above where the keyboard lands.

What is mocked here versus what is real

  • Real: your titles and authors, the three books with actual listening time, the chapter marks the player already draws, and every issue number.
  • Mocked: positions and times on the covers, the search result counts, and the cover art itself (flat panels, not your real images).
  • Not designed yet: settings, library picker, the update screen, and the empty and error states of all of the above — the empty state is usually where a TV app falls over, so it is worth a pass of its own.
  • Each section is one PR's worth of work. The rail (#58) comes first because the Home, detail and search frames all assume it.
