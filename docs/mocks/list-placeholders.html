Lists and grids: grey cards in the real shape while they load

Today Home shows "Loading…" until its shelves arrive. Library, Series, Collections, Authors and Narrators show "Loading series…" and the like, and Latest Episodes shows "Loading episodes…". Instead, each screen draws grey cards in the shape of the cards it is about to show: shelves on Home, a grid on the browse screens, rows on Latest Episodes. They are still (no shimmer) and cannot take focus, so the side menu keeps focus until real cards arrive and replace them in place. The screen's own title is real from the first frame. One shared placeholder component serves all of them.
