  • Nothing is invented structurally: every box maps to a field already in ListeningStatsResponse, which today's screen ignores four fifths of.
  • Average per day keeps its own tile, fifth in the row, and the weekday panel shows where that average comes from.
  • Focus: tiles, both panels and each session are focus targets, so the D-pad has somewhere to go; RIGHT off the rail lands on Total listened.
  • If it ever does not fit (a long locale string, a 7-day week that wraps), the whole content column scrolls rather than silently clipping.
